Read this first — five facts that shape the price

  1. You will be the first live 1-bedroom in the community. The Airbnb map box around the marina holds ~74 listings, but the genuine Rashid Yachts & Marina pool is eight units: seven 2–3-bedrooms in Seagate / Marina Views and one Sunridge 1BR (832 sq ft, top floor, 0 reviews) that is unavailable on every date through March on Airbnb and on Booking.com. No 1BR comp means no price anchor in guests' minds either — the listing has to explain the area.
  2. The real 1BR comps are the sea-view towers in Dubai Maritime City, 1.1 km away: ~490 guest-total per night in late September, 710–725 in early October, 850–1,130 in early November, 890–1,620 in late November. They carry 13–15 reviews; Sema starts at zero.
  3. The cruise thesis is off this winter. MSC, Costa, AIDA, TUI, Celestyal and Explora cancelled their 2026-27 Gulf seasons; only Aroya (Jan–Mar) and a few transit calls remain at Mina Rashid. MSC World Europa returns in December 2027. Price nothing for cruise demand until then.
  4. Supply is up, demand is recovering. Dubai 1BR Airbnb supply is +32% year on year (10,772 active; ADR AED 725; occupancy 43%); hotel occupancy fell to 56% in H1 2026 after the February regional shock and is expected to recover from Q4. Early Q4 bookings show ADR +17% and Russia–UAE flights triple by late October. Expect a real but partial winter.
  5. Position ~25% under Port de la Mer, ~30% under at NYE. Port de la Mer has a Burj Khalifa view, a beach and a Guest Favourite badge. Sunridge sells newness and water — not a landmark.

How the money works

Since August 2026 all of Sema's listings are on Airbnb's 15.5% host-only fee: guests pay no service fee, so YOU SET = GUEST SEES, and you net the posted price × 0.83725 (15.5% plus 5% VAT on the fee). The cleaning fee (AED 200), Dubai Tourism Dirham (AED 15 per night, holiday-home "Deluxe" tier) and 5% VAT are added as line items on top. A guest booking two October nights at 600 sees 1,200 + 200 cleaning + 30 Tourism Dirham + VAT; you receive 1,172 for the room and cleaning.

Comp prices in this document are guest-total per night for a 2-night stay (posted + cleaning ÷ 2), because that is what a scan sees. Add about 100 to any YOU SET number before comparing it to a comp.

Launch playbook

  1. Permits first. Confirm handover and furnishing; get the building NOC from the Sunridge owners' association / Emaar Community Management; apply for the DET holiday-home permit (≈AED 1,520 registration + AED 370 per bedroom per year). Note ECM's rule that an operator staff member is present at concierge for every check-in in its communities — ask the co-host how they comply at Port de la Mer.
  2. Instant Book on, Smart Pricing off. Port de la Mer converts 5.6% of views on Request-to-Book with a 66% response rate; a 0-review listing cannot afford that. Require government ID and a good track record instead.
  3. Turn on Airbnb's new-listing promotion (20% off the first three bookings) for October only. Three fast bookings at 480 net beat an empty October at 600 — the reviews are the asset. Switch it off on Nov 1.
  4. Load the calendar with minimum stays: 2 nights default · 3 nights National Day, F1 and Jan 1–15 · 4 nights Christmas, pre-NYE and NYE. Discounts: 7+ nights −10%, 14+ −15%, 28+ −22%.
  5. Title and photos sell the water and the newness: "Brand-new Emaar 1BR · Sea & Marina View · Rashid Yachts & Marina". First five photos: balcony/sea at golden hour, the canal pool, the promenade, the living room, the bedroom. Say "15 min to DXB, 10 min to Old Dubai (Al Seef, Gold Souk, Al Shindagha), Spinneys 2 min". State the view honestly.
  6. List on Booking.com in parallel. The community's 2BRs sell there; the other Sunridge 1BR is there; Mina Rashid has more Booking.com than Airbnb recognition today.
  7. Cross-sell from the other two flats. When Port de la Mer or MJL is booked, answer the enquiry with the Sunridge link at the lower price — the fastest source of the first reviews.
  8. Add the listing to the pricing bot the day it goes live (config recipe in the RUNBOOK): map-bounds search URL, Maritime City 1BR watchlist, a −10..0% band until reviews land.
  9. Re-scan before Nov 1 to lock the winter, and before Feb 1 to extend the calendar to a rolling 12 months.
